Privacy Policy for MONSOW POS Owner

Effective date: 26 October 2026

MONSOW POS Owner is a business dashboard application for authorised owners and managers using a MONSOW POS installation. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled by the app

To sign in, the app sends the Company ID, Branch ID, username and password entered by the user to the MONSOW POS main server at pos.monsowpos.com. The password is used for sign-in and is not stored by the app after the sign-in request.

After sign-in, the app retrieves business information needed for the Owner dashboard, including orders, sales and payment totals, stock information, invoices and operational reports. This information is supplied by the business's MONSOW POS server.

The app stores the selected Company ID, Branch ID, username, owner display name, a random device identifier and licence/trial information on the device. The device identifier is used only for app licensing and activation. A short-lived sign-in session is retained only for the active session and is removed when the user logs out.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorised user, provide the Owner dashboard and reports, operate licensing, and protect the service. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, location services, contacts, camera or microphone.

Sharing and transfers

The app communicates with the MONSOW POS main server when the user signs in or requests dashboard data. Business reporting may be processed through the business's configured reporting infrastructure. Information is not sold for advertising or marketing purposes.

Security and retention

Connections to the main server use HTTPS. Access is controlled by the Company ID, Branch ID and authorised account. Server-held business data is managed by the business operating the POS installation. Local app information remains on the device until it is changed, the user logs out, app data is cleared, or the app is removed.

Your choices and contact

You can log out at any time and remove locally stored information by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. For requests about server-held business data or privacy questions, contact the MONSOW POS account administrator or the business that provided your MONSOW POS installation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
